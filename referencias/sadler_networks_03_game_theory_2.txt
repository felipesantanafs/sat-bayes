Tools: Kakutani’s Fixed Point Theorem
Theorem (Kakutani)
Let f : A ⇒A be a correspondence, i.e. x ∈A =⇒f(x) ⊂A,
satisfying:
• A is a non-empty compact and convex subset of a ﬁnite
dimensional Euclidean space
• f(x) is non-empty for all x ∈A
• f(x) is convex valued
• f(x) has a closed graph, i.e. (xn, yn) →(x, y) with
yn ∈f(xn) implies y ∈f(x)
Then f has a ﬁxed point: there exists x ∈A such that x ∈f(x)

Equilibrium Existence in Inﬁnite Games
A similar theorem gives existence of pure strategy equilibria in
inﬁnite games
Theorem (Debreu, Glicksburg, Fan)
Consider an inﬁnite normal form game (N, {Si}i∈N, {ui}i∈N)
such that for each i ∈N:
• Si is compact and convex
• ui(si, s−i) is continuous in s−i
• ui(si, s−i) is continuous and concave in si
Then a pure strategy Nash Equilibrium exists.

X ≻y) if we can reach X through y
