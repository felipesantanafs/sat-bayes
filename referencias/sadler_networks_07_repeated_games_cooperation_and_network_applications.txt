Equilibria of Finitely-Repeated Games
Theorem
Consider the repeated game GT(δ) for T < ∞. If the stage
game G has a unique pure strategy equilibrium σ∗, then GT has
a unique SPE in which σ∗is played every period.
The proof follows the same logic as in the Prisoners’ Dilemma
example
By backward induction, at time T the unique outcome is σ∗, and
taking this as given, we can iterate to construct the unique SPE
